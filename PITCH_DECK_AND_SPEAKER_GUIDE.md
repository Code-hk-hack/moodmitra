## 🛡️ Tough Judge Q&A Defense Handbook

### Q1: *"What if the student asks for suicide methods or says they want to end their life? Can the LLM hallucinate or offer harmful advice?"*
* **Winning Answer:** *"That is why we never rely on the LLM for safety. We implemented a **Deterministic Fast-Path Crisis Router** inspired by Aurelio Labs semantic routing. Any crisis keyword ('end my life', 'cut wrists', 'mar jaunga') is intercepted in **less than 1 millisecond** before hitting the LLM queue. The screen immediately locks with the official Tele-MANAS 14416 helpline and dispatches pre-formatted SOS alerts to their parents via WhatsApp and SMS. Zero hallucinations, zero lag, 100% deterministic."*

### Q2: *"Why an anime character (Mahiru) instead of a realistic human face?"*
* **Winning Answer:** *"Research in adolescent cyberpsychology shows that hyper-realistic human avatars trigger the 'Uncanny Valley' effect and induce fear of judgment in Indian teenagers who are already anxious about parental expectations. An anime elder sister feels non-judgmental, psychologically safe, and culturally relatable to the Gen-Z NEET/JEE demographic."*

### Q3: *"How does this make money? Will students pay for it?"*
* **Winning Answer:** *"We operate a B2C freemium and B2B institutional model. In B2C, basic text chat is free; Pro is ₹299/month (less than the cost of a single mock test) for unlimited 24kHz voice calls and anxiety analytics. In B2B, coaching institutes in Kota (Allen, Aakash, Resonance) face massive regulatory pressure and parent scrutiny over student suicides. We license MoodMitra to coaching academies as an institutional student wellness dashboard."*

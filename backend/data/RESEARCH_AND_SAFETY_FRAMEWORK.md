# MoodMitra: Clinical Evidence, Ethics & Safety Framework
*Compiled from WHO, UNICEF, PubMed, NIST AI RMF, APA, and Indian MoHFW guidelines*

## 1. Indian Adolescent Context & Key Statistics (For Judge Defense)
- **Academic Stress Factor:** Jayanthi et al. (2015, PubMed) demonstrated that acute academic stress increases depression risk in Indian adolescents by **2.4x**.
- **Prevalence:** JEHP (2024) survey found that **86% of Indian NEET and JEE aspirants** report severe academic anxiety and chronic fear of disappointing parental expectations.
- **Emotional Intelligence vs. Performance:** Pradhan & Dash (2026) revealed that emotional regulation strongly correlates with examination persistence and cognitive recall ($r = 0.797$).
- **The Stigma Barrier:** Over 75% of Indian youth suffering from exam anxiety never consult school counselors or clinicians due to fear of social judgment and familial shame.

## 2. Ethical Guidelines & AI Governance Alignment
### A. UNICEF Policy Guidance on AI for Children
- **Child-Centered & Age-Appropriate:** Conversational tone avoids complex clinical jargon; uses friendly, elder-sibling language.
- **Privacy by Design:** Zero persistent biometric storage; audio buffers discarded immediately after Web Speech/Gnani synthesis.
- **No Romantic or Exploitative Mechanics:** Companions (Mahiru / male avatar) are strictly platonic study buddies without gamified affection meters or emotional dependency traps.

### B. WHO Ethics and Governance of AI for Health & APA Digital Health Standards
- **Explicit Non-Clinical Boundary:** Clear, persistent disclaimer that MoodMitra is an *emotional first-aid companion*, not a certified psychotherapist, psychiatrist, or medical diagnostic tool.
- **Never Diagnose:** AI instructions strictly forbid delivering DSM-5 / ICD-11 diagnostic labels (e.g., "You have Major Depressive Disorder"). Instead, it validates emotional states and teaches self-regulation.

## 3. Crisis Escalation Protocol (Tele-MANAS & National Network)

1. **Immediate State Shift:** Avatar transitions from idle to `urgent_listening` (attentive, compassionate posture).
2. **Immediate Safety Response:** Conversation is steered directly to certified crisis support.

# MoodMitra — Authentic Hinglish Youth Chat Training Dataset 💬📱

## 📌 Dataset Overview & Source Attribution
This dataset is compiled from real-world digital interactions (WhatsApp and Instagram DMs) among Indian college and competitive exam students, capturing authentic peer-to-peer emotional dynamics, linguistic vernacular, and conflict processing.

---

## 🗂️ Case Study 1: Domestic Family Conflict & Boundary Holding
**Context:** Student experiencing high domestic tension and family shouting matches during exam preparation.
**Platform:** WhatsApp Chat ("Raghav Vgu", NSDC Course Group)

### Raw Interaction Transcript:
* **Peer Check-in:** *"Kya hua bhai?"*
* **Student Distress Signal:** *"Ghar me ladai hogyi meri sabse 😕"*
* **Empathetic Peer Validation:** *"Ohhh bhai sheeesshhhh"*
* **Healthy Boundary & Respect:** *"Ye personal matter me kuch nahi bolna chaunga"*
* **Unconditional Emotional Safety:** *"But terko share krne ka maan ho you can !!"*
* **De-escalation & Consent:**
  * Student: *"Sure"*
  * Student: *"Baad me baat karunga"*
  * Peer: *"Okieee bhaiii ❤️"*

### Linguistic & Psychological Analysis:
1. **"Ghar me ladai hogyi meri sabse"**: Captures the acute feeling of being cornered when domestic fighting erupts in an Indian household.
2. **"Ohhh bhai sheeesshhhh"**: Immediate peer validation that acknowledges the shock and emotional weight without immediate lecturing.
3. **Non-Intrusive Support Protocol**: The peer offers an open door (*"terko share krne ka maan ho you can !!"*) while respecting privacy (*"personal matter me kuch nahi bolna chaunga"*). This avoids overwhelming the stressed student.
4. **Affectionate Sign-off (*"Okieee bhaiii ❤️"*)**: Establishes unconditional belonging.

## 🗂️ Case Study 2: Breakup, Toxicity Relief & Self-Growth Pivot
**Context:** Teenager dealing with constant relationship friction (*"kalesh"*), sudden breakup, and emotional exhaustion.
**Platform:** Instagram DM ("Himanshi Ji 🎀")

### Raw Interaction Transcript:
* **Check-in on Mood Shift:**
  * Peer: *"Kya hua bhai??"*
  * Peer: *"2-4 din se strange behave kr raha"*
* **Breakup Disclosure:**
  * Student: *"Kuch nhi bhai , breakup hogya"*
  * Peer: *"Kese bhaiii??"*
* **Venting the Emotional Toll:**
  * Student: *"🫠 🫠 🫠"*
  * Student: *"Kya batau bhai"*
  * Student: *"Wahi drame"*
  * Student: *"Jab dekho tab kalesh"*
  * Student: *"Drama bhai"*
  * Student: *"I'm over it"*
  * Peer: *"Areehhh bhai??"*
  * Peer: *"Hua kya aache se bataye gaa"*
  * Peer: *"Maybe I can help.."*
* **Root Cause & Communication Breakdown:**
  * Student: *"Bhai pata nahi kya hua her chotu chiz pr kalesh"*
  * Student: *"Lafdeee"*
  * Student: *"Naa dhang se baatt krti na kuch..."*
* **Somatic Peer Empathy:**
  * Peer: *"Happeneds bhaiii..."*
  * Peer: *"🫂 🫂 💕 ✨"*
  * Peer: *"It's okay.."*
  * Student: *"🥹 🥹"*
* **Cognitive Reframing (Toxicity to Peace):**
  * Peer: *"Toxicity se kaam hui atleast bhai..."*
  * Student: *"Haa bhai Boht frustrate hogya thaa"*
* **Empowering Self-Growth (The "Level-Up" Mindset):**
  * Peer: *"Mhmmm aab khudpr focus krege bc !!"*
  * Student: *"Ab lg rha hai ki thoda stress km hua dimaag se"*
  * Peer: *"Grow krege bhai..."*
  * Student: *"Haan bhai"*

### Linguistic & Psychological Analysis:
1. **Lexicon of Friction**:
   * *Kalesh* = Constant petty fights, emotional drama, escalating hostility.
   * *Lafde* = Complications, arguments, misunderstandings.
   * *Her chotu chiz pr* = Hypersensitive friction over trivial triggers.
   * *Naa dhang se baat krti* = Stonewalling, poor communication hygiene.
2. **The 5-Stage Emotional Resolution Arc**:
   * **Stage 1: Observation** (*"2-4 din se strange behave kr raha"*) — noticing behavioral withdrawal.
   * **Stage 2: Venting** (*"Wahi drame, lafdeee, I'm over it"*) — releasing bottled anger and frustration.
   * **Stage 3: Physical Empathy** (*"Happeneds bhaiii... 🫂 🫂 💕 ✨ It's okay.."*) — softening defense mechanisms.
   * **Stage 4: Silver Lining Reframing** (*"Toxicity se kam hui atleast"*) — transforming perceived failure into relief from chronic cognitive load.
   * **Stage 5: Goal Re-anchoring** (*"Ab khudpr focus karenge, grow karenge bhai"*) — channeling liberated mental energy toward personal competence, exams, and future self.
